# When Someone Says Yes

## Same day
1. **Get paid first.** Zelle request (US) / Bit or PayBox (Israel). Note: "Reelwise — {{Business}} — Starter, {{month}}".
2. Send the welcome message (below).
3. Add them to a "Clients" tab in your Google Sheet: name, package, price, renewal date, Instagram handle.

## Welcome message
> Welcome aboard, {{Name}}! 🎉 Here's what I need to get started:
> 1. Instagram access — easiest is Meta Business Suite → Settings → Partners → add me ({{your email}}). Or send login if you prefer.
> 2. 10–20 photos/videos of the place, team, products (a shared Google Drive folder works great).
> 3. Logo + top 3 things you want to promote + any deals coming up.
> 4. Anything you **don't** want posted.
>
> First 3 reels will be ready for your approval by {{date}}. Posting schedule: Mon / Wed / Fri.

## Weekly production (~1–1.5 hrs per client per week, mostly AI)
1. **Monday:** Claude writes the week's 3 reel ideas + captions + hashtags for the client.
2. Your agent generates the reels from their photos/videos.
3. Send to client for quick 👍 (first month only — after that, just post).
4. Schedule in Meta Business Suite (free).

## Monthly (5 days before renewal)
- 1-page report: reels posted, total views, follower growth, best reel.
- Message: "Here's your month! 📈 Renewing on {{date}} — anything you want more of next month?"
- Ask for a testimonial (founding clients) + a referral: "Know another owner who'd want this? I'll give you $50 off next month for each one who signs up."

## Content idea bank (works for most local businesses)
- Behind the scenes / process (making the food, the haircut, the treatment)
- Before → after
- "3 things you didn't know about {{Business}}"
- Meet the team / owner story
- Customer reaction / testimonial
- Promo / holiday special (Hanukkah, Christmas, New Year's)
- Trending audio + local Miami/Tel Aviv angle
