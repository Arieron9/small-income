Questions

Do I need to film anything?

No. We work from your existing photos and videos. If you want, you can send a few quick phone clips — we'll handle the rest.

Is there a contract?

No. Month-to-month, cancel anytime.

Do I approve the posts?

Yes — in the first month you approve every reel. After that, most clients let us post automatically.

Will this make me go viral?

We don't promise viral. We promise your page looks active, professional and on-brand every week — which is what new customers check before they visit.

Do you buy reviews?

Never. The Review Booster only helps you ask your real customers for honest reviews.
