# 15-Minute Sales Call

Goal of the call: **they say yes and pay the first month.** You talk 30%, they talk 70%.

## 1. Open (1 min)
> "Thanks for jumping on! Quick plan: I'll ask a few questions about the business, show you what I'd do, and if it's a fit I'll tell you the price. Sound good?"

## 2. Questions (5 min)
1. "How do most new customers find you right now?"
2. "Who handles Instagram today? How much time does it take?"
3. "What's your busiest / slowest day? What would you want more of?"
4. "Have you tried an agency or freelancer before? What happened?"

Write down their words — use them back in step 4.

## 3. Show (3 min)
- Their free sample reel + 2 more examples.
- "Here's what a month looks like": 12 reels, posted Mon/Wed/Fri, captions written, monthly numbers report.

## 4. Offer (2 min)
> "So you said {{their problem, their words}}. Here's how I'd fix it: I take Instagram completely off your plate — 3 reels a week, posted for you. It's normally $300 a month; since I'm taking 5 founding clients this month, your first month is $150. No contract, and if you don't like the first 3 reels you don't pay."

Then **stop talking** and wait.

## 5. Close (2 min)
> "Want to get started? I'll send you a Zelle request now, and you'll have your first 3 reels by {{date, ~5 days}}."

## Objections

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "Too expensive" | "Totally fair. That's $5 a day — if it brings in one extra customer a week it pays for itself. And the first month is half price with no contract." |
| "I need to think about it" | "Of course. What's the part you're unsure about?" (answer that). Then: "I'll hold the founding spot until Friday." |
| "My nephew does our Instagram" | "Nice — I can make him the reels and he just posts them, or I can handle it all so he's off the hook." |
| "Does Instagram even work?" | "For local businesses, Instagram + Google is the first thing people check. A dead page makes people think you're closed. We're not promising viral — we're promising you look alive and great every week." |
| "Can I see results first?" | "That's what the guarantee is for — 3 reels, you don't like them, you don't pay." |
| "Send me info" | "Sure — I'll email it. Can we put 10 minutes on Thursday to go over it?" (book it while on the call) |
