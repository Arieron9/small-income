# Cold Emails (English)

Replace `{{...}}`. Keep it short — owners read on their phone.

## Sending rules

- Use a **new agency Gmail**, not your personal/school email.
- Ramp: Day 1–3: 15–20/day · Day 4–7: 30/day · Week 2+: 50/day. Send by hand or with Gmail mail-merge (e.g. free tier of a Sheets mail-merge add-on).
- Best times: Tue–Thu, 8–10am or 2–4pm local.
- Every email ends with your mailing address + opt-out line (required by US law).
- Find emails: Google Maps listing → website → "Contact" page, or Instagram bio.
