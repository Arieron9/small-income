Funnel assumption (typical for cold outreach with a personal sample): 100 messages → 5–8 replies → 2–3 calls → 1 client.
So **~400–500 messages over 3–4 weeks gets you to 8 clients.** Week 1 realistic target: **1–2 clients.**

## The secret weapon: the free sample reel

Your own Instagram is new (30 followers) — that's fine, nobody buys based on your followers.
They buy when they **see a reel made about *their* business.** For every good lead:
1. Grab 3–5 photos/videos from their Instagram or Google Maps listing.
2. Have your agent make one 10–20s reel from them (CapCut free / your agent).
3. Send it in the first message: *"I made this for you — free, yours to post."*

Make the sample only for your best 10–15 leads per day; everyone else gets the plain email.

## Rules we follow

- Cold email: your physical address + "reply STOP to opt out" in every email (US CAN-SPAM). Ramp slowly (20/day → 50/day) so Gmail doesn't flag the new account.
- Israel: anti-spam law is stricter for email → use Instagram/WhatsApp DMs and calls there, one message + one follow-up max.
- Reviews add-on only asks **real customers** for honest reviews — never pay for or write reviews.
