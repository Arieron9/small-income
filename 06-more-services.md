# More Services — Save Owners Time or Get Them Customers

Every service below follows one rule: **sell an outcome the owner already wants (more customers or more free time), deliver it mostly with AI, and cost $0 in tools to start.**

## The service ladder

You don't sell everything at once. Every client walks up the ladder:

```
FREE sample (reel / profile audit)
   ↓
QUICK WIN — one-time $150–$400, done in 1–3 days   ← fast cash in week 1
   ↓
MONTHLY — $300–$800/mo recurring                   ← this is what gets you to $100/day
```

One-time projects pay you **this week**. Monthly clients pay you **every month**. You need both.

## Ranked list (best for you first)

### 1. Google Business Profile Tune-Up — *get more customers* ⭐ easiest to close
**What:** When people search "barber near me", Google Maps shows 3 businesses. Most owners have a messy profile: wrong categories, few photos, no posts, unanswered reviews. You fix it.
**Deliver (2–3 hrs, AI writes it):** correct categories and services, keyword-rich description, 15+ photos uploaded, 4 Google posts, Q&A section filled, replies to every review, hours and holiday hours.
**Price:** $200 one-time (₪700 in Israel). Upsell: $100/mo to post weekly + reply to reviews.
**Why it closes:** you can show the problem in 30 seconds: *"Here's you on Google Maps vs. the 3 shops above you."*
**Tools:** free (owner adds you as a manager on their profile).

### 2. Holiday Win-Back Campaign — *get more customers* ⭐ perfect for December
**What:** Every business has a list of old customers (phone numbers, emails, booking app) they never message. You write and send a holiday offer to them.
**Deliver (2–4 hrs):** offer idea ("20% off gift cards until Dec 31"), 1 WhatsApp/SMS message + 2 emails, a simple graphic in Canva; the owner sends it from their own phone or booking app (or you send it from their email account).
**Price:** $250 per campaign, or $150 + 10% of tracked sales from the promo code.
**Why it closes:** it's money in their pocket in 2 weeks, and it's the season for it.
**Rule:** only message customers who gave the business their contact info; include opt-out.

### 3. Listing Reels for Real Estate Agents — *save time + get customers* ⭐ Miami goldmine
**What:** Miami has thousands of realtors. Each new listing needs a reel. Most agents hate making them.
**Deliver (~45 min each, your agent does it):** 30–45s reel from the listing photos — music, price, key features, agent's branding — plus caption.
**Price:** $75 per listing, or $250/mo for 4 listings.
**Why it closes:** agents get paid thousands per sale; $75 is nothing. They repeat every listing. Find them on Instagram, Zillow, Realtor.com.

### 4. Instagram DM Auto-Reply — *save time + get customers*
**What:** People DM "price?" "are you open?" "how do I book?" and owners reply hours later (or never). You set up automatic replies.
**Deliver (2–3 hrs):** ManyChat free plan (free up to 1,000 contacts): FAQ replies, booking link, and "comment SPECIAL on this reel and get the offer by DM" automation.
**Price:** $250 setup + $50/mo upkeep. Natural add-on for your reels clients.

### 5. One-Page Website — *get more customers*
**What:** Lots of small businesses only have Instagram, or a broken 2015 website.
**Deliver (2–3 hrs — I build it, like your own site):** one mobile page with services, prices, photos, map, booking/WhatsApp button. Free hosting (GitHub Pages / Netlify). Client pays for their own domain (~$12/year).
**Price:** $300–$400 one-time, + $30/mo for updates.

### 6. Missed-Call / Booking Reminders — *save time*
**What:** No-shows and missed calls cost salons, clinics and trainers hundreds per week.
**Deliver:** turn on the reminder features in the booking app they already use (Square, Booksy, Vagaro, Fresha, Calendly) — most owners never set them up — plus written reminder/confirmation messages.
**Price:** $150 one-time. Fast, easy, owners feel the result in the first week.

### 7. Small-Business Admin Assistant (later)
**What:** Weekly done-for-you admin with AI: answering emails, chasing unpaid invoices, organizing receipts in Google Sheets, writing quotes.
**Price:** $300–500/mo. Good for solo entrepreneurs (contractors, coaches, cleaners). Needs more trust — sell this to clients who already know you.
